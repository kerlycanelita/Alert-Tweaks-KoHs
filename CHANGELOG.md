# Changelog

## [2.0.0]

### Added
- 14 alert cards: no totem in offhand, totems running low, enemy totem pops, armour breaking, weapon or tool breaking, shield disabled, cooldown ready, harmful effect, effect running out, too hungry to sprint, dangerous fall, supplies running low, inventory full and server not responding.
- Per-alert switch, sound and threshold, with a Test button; card size, time on screen, volume and a crimson edge flash for danger alerts.
- Five looks per alert: card, compact, icon, text and banner.
- Position editor: every alert can be dragged anywhere on your real screen; cards dropped together stack; a ten-second tutorial shows how.
- Player alerts: a watch list of names, with a card and their face when they join or leave the server (read from the player list only).
- Eight threat indicator styles: Classic, Arc, Chevrons, Slash, Radar, Weapon, Edge and Shard.
- Builds for Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.
- Spanish (es_es and the six regional variants).

### Changed
- New settings screen: five pages, an option tree, a live miniature of your own HUD, a totem entrance once per session and a totem sweep between pages. Works from the title screen and in game, at every GUI size.
- One source tree for every version (Stonecutter) instead of a copy per version.
- New icon: a sweating heart under attack from PvP gear in front of a Nether portal (animated on Modrinth).
